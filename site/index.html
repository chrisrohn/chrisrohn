Settings

Signing in uses YouTube API Services; see the Privacy Policy and Terms. Anyone can listen here. Sign in with Google and the thumbs appear: 👍 adds the track to a year playlist in your own YouTube library right away (curator: <year> | Indie Discotheque; guests: <year> Picks from chrisrohn.com), with Undo for a few seconds. 👎 hides it for good. Your thumbs are mirrored to a hidden app-data file in your own Google Drive, so every device you sign into stays in step. Nothing else is touched.

Google sign-in lasts an hour at a time and renews itself the next time you tap a thumb. Sign out only forgets this device; to disconnect the site from your Google account entirely, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Duplicate check

Every 👍 first checks the target playlist, so the same song is never added twice. The daily build also scans all year playlists for the same video added twice, a second upload of the same song, or one song filed under two years.
