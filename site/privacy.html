1. What this site is

chrisrohn.com ("the Site") is a personal new-music discovery feed for the Indie Discotheque radio station. It is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We run no servers of our own, we do not operate user accounts, and we do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Visitors who just listen

  • No personal data is collected by us. There is no sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • Browser storage. Your filter choices (sources, sort order, search text) are saved in your browser's local storage so they persist between visits. This never leaves your device.
  • Hosting logs. The Site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP address and user agent) under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.
  • Embedded YouTube player. Tracks play through YouTube's embedded player. When a video loads, YouTube/Google may set cookies and collect data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2.2 Curators who sign in with Google

Anyone may use "Sign in with Google". The Site uses YouTube API Services to let a signed-in user file tracks into playlists in their own YouTube library: the curator's approvals go to the "<year> | Indie Discotheque" playlists, and any other signed-in user's approvals go to "<year> Picks from chrisrohn.com" playlists in that user's library. No user can write to another user's playlists. When you sign in, the Site requests permission for:

  • Basic profile (openid, email): your email address, display name, and profile picture. Used only to show who is signed in and to decide whether the account is the curator (Indie Discotheque playlists) or a guest (your own "Picks" playlists).
  • App configuration data in Google Drive (drive.appdata scope): used to store one small JSON file, in a hidden folder only this Site can see, listing the tracks you have thumbed up or down (artist, title, YouTube video ID, year, timestamp). This is what keeps your ratings in step across your devices. The Site cannot see, read, or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube scope): used only to list your playlists, create a year playlist or the unlisted "Skipped" playlist if one is missing, add a track to a playlist when you press 👍 or 👎, and remove that entry if you press Undo. The Site never reads your watch history, subscriptions, comments, or private videos, and never posts, comments, or uploads on your behalf.

Where this data lives. The Google access token, your email, name, and picture, and the IDs of your year playlists are stored only in your browser's local storage on the device you signed in from. The list of tracks you rated is kept in that local storage and mirrored to the hidden app-data file in your own Google Drive described above; deleting that file (Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → this app → Delete hidden app data) or revoking access removes it. The access token expires after about one hour. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any server we control, because there is none; all YouTube API calls go directly from your browser to Google.

Sharing. We do not share, sell, or transfer any Google user data to third parties. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the playlist functions described above.

Retention and deletion. Press Sign out to revoke the token and clear your identity from the browser, or use ⚙ → Clear local state to erase everything the Site stored. You can also revoke the Site's access at any time from your Google security settings. Because we hold no copies, revoking access removes all of it.

2.3 The daily feed build

Once a day an automated job builds the feed from public data: Last.fm listening statistics for the Indie Discotheque station account, public MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz release data, Bandcamp, Deezer, and music-blog RSS feeds. It also reads the curator's public YouTube year playlists and the unlisted "Skipped" playlist (by ID) to avoid re-suggesting tracks that were already saved or skipped. Artist names and track titles derived from those playlists are stored in the Site's public source repository. This concerns the curator's own playlists only; no other user's YouTube data is read.

3. YouTube API Services disclosures

  • This Site uses YouTube API Services. By using the Site you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

The Site itself sets no cookies. It uses browser local storage as described above. Embedded YouTube players and Google sign-in may set their own cookies, governed by Google's policies.
